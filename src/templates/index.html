how the score works

the detector returns a combined score mapped so that 0% = likely human and 100% = likely ai. that scale stays consistent across single-file and batch flows.

tips

if a track scores high before remaster but still feels fine to you, it may just have very clean editing or heavy processing. use the score as a second opinion, not a moral verdict.

aggressive mode is better for stubborn tracks.
